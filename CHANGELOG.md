# Changelog

All notable changes to *Cuộc sống và con người* are documented here. The format follows the living-book rule: each version changes only the chapters it lists.

## [1.10.0] — Deferred fixes: Chapters 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8

This release touches several chapters; each change is small and listed below.

- **Ch. 1:** the "counting blades of grass" example, which is Rawls's and not in Wolf's book, replaced by Wolf's own Sisyphus Fulfilled case (after Richard Taylor, whose conclusion she rejects), with page references; Wolf's formula quoted with its page; the phrase "leap of faith" noted as not occurring in Kierkegaard's published works (McKinnon 1993); Camus's closing sentence given in French.
- **Ch. 8:** newer evidence on companion and general-purpose chatbots: short-term reductions in loneliness (De Freitas et al. 2025), a four-week randomized trial with ChatGPT in which more voluntary use went with worse outcomes (Fang et al. 2025, preprint; correlational), and a survey of student Replika users (Maples et al. 2024), each with its limits.
- **Ch. 7:** Vietnamese name of Cuong Tu Nguyen (Nguyễn Tự Cường) added.
- **Ch. 1, 4, 5, 6, 7:** SEP edition, publication and revision dates moved out of the text into the references; in Ch. 4 the higher-brain claim is now attributed to Omelianchuk and DeGrazia and cited.
- 4 new sources.

## [1.9.0] — Women thinkers: Beauvoir, Murdoch, Weil, Nussbaum

This is the release planned as 1.7.0. It is numbered 1.9.0 because 1.8.0 shipped first.

- **Ch. 4:** Simone de Beauvoir on living one's finitude with passion, her criticism of Heidegger, and old age (*La Vieillesse*); a new row in the positions map, with her own later self-criticism.
- **Ch. 5:** Iris Murdoch on attention and the example of M and D; Blum's points that she is not a virtue theorist and that she neglects the social sources of moral formation.
- **Ch. 6:** Beauvoir on authentic love as the mutual recognition of two freedoms, against Sartre's love as conflict.
- **Ch. 7:** Simone Weil on affliction (*malheur*) as social, psychological and physical, and its supernatural use, with the charge that she glorifies suffering and Thomas's defence; Martha Nussbaum on emotions as responses to value, with the standard objections to judgment theories and the reply to them.
- 14 new sources; the SEP and IEP entries, the Weil text, the Cleary entry and the Crossref records were opened and checked.

## [1.5.0] — Chapter 2: debate 2.2 deepened

- **Debate 2.2, "what should 'human nature' mean today?":**
  - Hull's sceptical argument (1986): species are variable historical entities, so there is no essence shared by all humans;
  - Machery's narrower "nomological" reply, with its main difficulty (separating evolution from social learning);
  - contemporary responses: Sterelny (reconstructions succeed at the cost of explanatory salience), Dupré (a process view);
  - **correction:** Nathan (2024) was previously described as rejecting every candidate; he in fact argues that discarding human nature as a myth is misguided and calls it an "epistemological indicator";
  - why researchers disagree (the split does not follow disciplinary lines).
- The assessment now notes there is no automatic move from explanatory claims to normative ones (Roughley, SEP).
- 4 new sources: Hull 1986; Downes & Machery 2013; Hannon & Lewens 2018; Mameli 2024 (further reading).

## [1.1.0] — Literature-review corrections only

This release applies the systematic literature review completed on 9 November 2026 to v1.0. It corrects citations and dates and replaces the bibliography with a verified, deduplicated `references.bib`. **No new arguments were added.**

### The 12 must-fix corrections

1. **Terror management theory founding citation (Ch. 4) corrected.** The widely circulating *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* citation for this theory is erroneous and is no longer used. Correct citation: Greenberg, Pyszczynski & Solomon (1986), "The causes and consequences of a need for self-esteem: A terror management theory", in Baumeister (ed.), *Public Self and Private Self*, Springer, pp. 189–212, DOI 10.1007/978-1-4613-9564-5_10.
2. **Many Labs 4 dating (Ch. 4) corrected to 2022.** Klein et al. (2022), *Collabra: Psychology* 8(1): 35271, DOI 10.1525/collabra.35271 — not 2020. Terror management theory is now presented as a hypothesis, not an established fact, given this failed replication of the mortality salience effect.
3. **Frankl title history (Ch. 1 and 7) corrected.** German original 1946, *Ein Psycholog erlebt das Konzentrationslager*; English 1959 was *From Death-Camp to Existentialism*; the title *Man's Search for Meaning* dates only from 1962.
4. **Aristotle dating (Ch. 1, 5, 6) corrected.** *Nicomachean Ethics* and *De Anima* have no publication year. The book now uses "c. 340–330 BCE" and "c. 350 BCE" respectively and cites by Bekker numbers.
5. **Laozi / Upanishads / Gita dating (Ch. 1, 7) corrected to composite ranges.** *Daodejing* c. 4th–3rd century BCE; principal Upanishads c. 800–300 BCE; *Bhagavad Gita* generally 2nd–1st century BCE, scholarly range 5th–2nd century BCE.
6. **Libet and Soon full citations added (Ch. 3).** Libet et al. (1983), *Brain* 106(3): 623–642, DOI 10.1093/brain/106.3.623; Soon et al. (2008), *Nature Neuroscience* 11(5): 543–545, DOI 10.1038/nn.2112.
7. **Henrich et al. WEIRD pages corrected (Ch. 2).** Target article pp. 61–83, not 61–135 (61–135 includes commentary). *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 33(2–3), 61–83 (2010), DOI 10.1017/S0140525X0999152X.
8. **Bowlby and Hume titles corrected (Ch. 6).** Bowlby is *Attachment and Loss, Vol. 1: Attachment* (1969), not "Attachment, 1969" alone. Hume's work is *A Treatise of Human Nature*, not *Treatise of Human Understanding*.
9. **Beauchamp & Childress 1st edition (Ch. 5) corrected.** Printed 1978, copyright 1979 — cited as 1979 with that note; 8th edition 2019 verified (Oxford University Press, 512 pp., ISBN 9780190640873).
10. **Tedeschi & Calhoun pages corrected (Ch. 7).** Pp. 455–471, not 472. DOI 10.1002/jts.2490090305.
11. **Williams pages corrected (Ch. 4).** Pp. 82–100, not 82–101. In *Problems of the Self*, Cambridge University Press, 1973.
12. **Fischer dating corrected (Ch. 4).** Cited as 2019, paperback 2020 — not only as 2020. *Death, Immortality, and Meaning in Life*, Oxford University Press, ISBN 978-0-19-092114-9.
